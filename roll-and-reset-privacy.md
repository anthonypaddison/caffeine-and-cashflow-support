Roll & Reset is a task-selection app provided by Caffeine & Cashflow. This
policy describes version 1.0.0 for Android, package
`com.anthonypaddison.rollandreset`.

## Information handled by the app

Roll & Reset does not require an account and does not operate an app-owned
server. It does not include advertising, analytics tracking or in-app purchases
in this version. Task selection and D20 rolls happen on your device.

The app saves your selected capacity, selected task list, onboarding status and
recent task history in private app storage on your device. Task history contains
the task family, list, D20 result, when the task was shown and, when applicable,
when it was completed. This information is used locally to avoid unhelpful
repetition and respect task cooldowns. It is not sent to Caffeine & Cashflow.

Clearing the app's storage through Android or uninstalling the app removes this
local information. Android backup or device-transfer services may copy app data
according to your device and account settings. Those services are controlled by
your device provider, not by Caffeine & Cashflow.

## Device features

The app may use vibration or haptic feedback when a die lands or a task is
completed. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone,
contacts or shared media library.

## External services and links

Google Play and Android may separately process store, installation, device,
backup or diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings.
This policy does not replace Google's privacy policy.

The Settings page can open this policy in your browser or start an email to the
support address. Opening a website makes a normal web request. If you send an
email, the recipient receives your email address and the information you choose
to include.

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes.
