# Security policy

Do not report vulnerabilities through public GitHub Issues.

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/anthonypaddison/caffeine-and-cashflow-support/security/advisories/new) where available, or email [caffeineandcashflowsupport@gmail.com](mailto:caffeineandcashflowsupport@gmail.com).

Include the affected app and context, concise reproduction steps, observed impact, and whether the behaviour is repeatable. Remove customer content, credentials and personal information from screenshots or logs.

Caffeine and Cashflow does not operate a bug-bounty programme, offer financial rewards, or promise 24/7 incident response.
