Roll & Reset helps you choose one useful task when everything feels like too
much. Pick your current capacity, choose what needs attention and roll the D20
for one manageable action.

The app has no accounts, streaks or guilt. Your choices and recent task history
stay on your device.

Roll & Reset is being prepared for Android testing.
