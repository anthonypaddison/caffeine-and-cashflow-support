Timed Highlight is a small Confluence Cloud macro that keeps text highlighted through a chosen expiry date and displays it as ordinary text afterwards.

## Use Timed Highlight

1. Edit a Confluence page.
2. Insert `Timed Highlight`.
3. Enter the text.
4. Choose the expiry date.
5. Publish or update the page.

The highlight remains active through the selected expiry date and appears as ordinary text afterwards.

## Current limitations

- Timed Highlight supports plain text only.
- It does not convert existing selected rich text.
- Links, mentions and rich formatting inside the macro are not supported in V1.
- A page already open across the expiry boundary may need refreshing.
