Dice & Disorder is a tabletop dice-rolling app provided by Caffeine & Cashflow.
This policy describes version 1.0.0 for Android, package
`com.caffeineandcashflow.diceanddisorder`.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not operate an app-owned server.
It does not include advertising, analytics tracking or in-app purchases in this
version. Dice rolls are calculated on your device; the app does not send your
rolls, preset names or settings to Caffeine & Cashflow.

Settings, named dice presets and recent roll results are saved in private app
storage on your device. You can delete individual presets and choose how many
recent rolls to keep. Setting history retention to zero removes the stored roll
history. Clearing the app's storage through Android removes its local settings,
presets and history.

Android backup or device-transfer services may copy app data according to your
device and account settings. These services are controlled by your device
provider, not by Caffeine & Cashflow. A backup may restore local app data when
you reinstall or change devices; manage backups through your device settings.

## Optional motion input

Shake-to-roll is off by default. If you enable it, the app uses accelerometer
readings to recognise a shake. Readings are processed locally to control rolling;
they are not saved as motion history or sent to Caffeine & Cashflow. The app does
not request location, camera, microphone, shared-storage or physical-activity
recognition access.

## External services and links

Google Play and Android may separately process store, installation, device,
backup or diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings.
This policy does not replace Google's privacy policy.

Open-source licence notices can be read inside the app. Choosing to open a
licence webpage displays a confirmation before opening an external browser.
External websites, including this GitHub Pages support site, receive normal web
requests and handle information under their own privacy policies.

## Support and privacy enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to
include, and use it to respond to your enquiry. Do not send passwords, private
keys or unnecessary sensitive personal information. GitHub support issues are
public; use email for private enquiries.

For product help, email [support@caffeineandcashflow.co.uk](mailto:support@caffeineandcashflow.co.uk).
For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us,
email [privacy@caffeineandcashflow.co.uk](mailto:privacy@caffeineandcashflow.co.uk).

## Future changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. Optional paid
cosmetics have not been implemented in this version; any future purchase or
account functionality will need an updated description of its data handling.
